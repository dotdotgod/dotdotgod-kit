# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Software developers and AI-assisted engineering teams who want coding agents to understand project rules, documentation, active intent, and verification context without broad repository scans.

## Product Purpose

dotdotgod provides a durable, reviewable project-memory structure shared across coding agents. Its brand content should explain the method concretely and make documentation structure, bounded context, and agent workflows easy to understand.

## Brand Personality

Technical, precise, and direct.

## Anti-references

No specific visual style is prohibited. Avoid decoration that obscures technical content or makes paths and commands difficult to read.

## Design Principles

- Show real project structures and commands instead of generic technology imagery.
- Make hierarchy and relationships understandable at a glance.
- Keep technical content accurate enough to reuse outside the visual.
- Give dotdotgod brand content a consistent, recognizable visual system.
- Prefer focused context over dense information dumps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast text and preserve legibility when thumbnails are reduced to small feed cards. Do not rely on color alone to communicate hierarchy.
