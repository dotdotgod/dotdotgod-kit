# Impact Graph Explorer Surface Brief

Mode: Operate

Audience: developers and AI-assisted engineering teams reviewing which maintained files may be affected by a change.

Job: select one or more changed files, distinguish traceability evidence from inferred or structural relationships, inspect score explanations, and re-root without losing context.

Primary task: identify the next documents, implementations, and tests that deserve review.

Constraints: local-only server, bounded payload, no editing, no graph-distance-as-score implication, keyboard-accessible ranked-list parity, reduced-motion support.
