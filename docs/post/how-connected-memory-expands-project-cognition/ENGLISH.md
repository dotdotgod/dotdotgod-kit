# Connected Memory Expands Our Sense of a Project

**Status:** DEV Community draft  
**Language:** [한국어](README.md)

Project memory organized around a purpose integrates new knowledge with existing ideas and evidence. It reveals what is known, what still needs investigation, and which action should follow. Over time, those connections develop more precise questions and richer judgment criteria.

In this article, cognitive expansion means developing the ability to learn and judge toward a purpose. New knowledge connects to what the project already knows. The relationship exposes another question and leads to an action. The result returns to project memory and improves the next round of learning and judgment.

## Reading Memory Improved the Resolution of Questions

`book` develops reading notes into personal interpretations and follow-up research. An early note in the project contained an intuition about the role of fact-checking. The report turned that intuition into research questions. Further research separated effects on factual beliefs, political attitudes, sharing behavior, voting behavior, and the speaker's attitude toward truth.

Those distinctions expanded the investigation. A question about fact-checking led to research on prebunking and media literacy, journalism and institutions, platform design, deliberation, and the Korean social context.

The relationships between `memo`, `report`, `research`, and `record` preserve where each question began and which sources informed it. A claim without a source leads to more research, and the result updates the earlier interpretation.

The project developed from summarizing one topic into examining outcomes, levels of evidence, scope of application, and the questions that remain open.

## Investment Memory Developed More Dimensions of Judgment

`stock` connects market interpretations, backtests, investment theses, and actual trade records to evaluate personal investment decisions. It converted an interpretation of a market rebound into explicit backtest rules. The base strategy produced 19 trades and a 63.16% win rate, while average trade return and net return after costs were negative. Average losses from stopped trades were larger than average gains from target exits.

A stricter variant produced better numbers, but the full sample contained ten trades and the target market regime contained one. The project recorded the variant as an exploratory scenario chosen after viewing the results and compared it with results from other semiconductor securities and markets.

The report preserved results and limitations. Strategy documents updated action conditions and risk rules. Position documents managed security-specific theses and invalidation conditions. Journals and trading data connected decisions with actual execution facts.

The project's market sense expanded from direction and win rate to expectancy, loss structure, sample size, cost, reproducibility, and deployment conditions. That broader judgment led to withheld deployment and further verification.

## Dispute Memory Made Resolution Paths Concrete

`roof-claim-now` organizes the facts and evidence of a dispute involving a shared rooftop and access for leak-repair work. It connects facts, hypotheses, open inquiries, factual evidence, legal grounds, and external filings through stable identifiers.

The structure divided the case into shared-space use, access for repairs, leak causation, structural load, drainage and waterproofing, administrative authority, civil remedies, and future operating procedures.

Building-related facts became questions for the administrative authority. The applicability of fire-safety rules became a question for the fire authority. Access and interference informed the review of civil remedies. Future repair procedures developed into operating terms for mediation.

Evidence documents recorded the facts supported by each source and the scope of that support. Legal research recorded applicability conditions. External outputs connected established facts to the questions each institution was being asked to investigate.

The project developed from describing an experience into seeing facts, evidence, applicability conditions, decision-making authorities, and available remedies together.

## Connected Memory Develops Project Sense

When a question has no research attached, it becomes a research topic. When a claim has no source, primary-source review becomes the next task. When a backtest has too little evidence, verification continues. When trade records conflict, the ledger must be reconciled. When a fact lacks support, another source must be collected. When institutional authority remains unclear, jurisdiction becomes the next question.

The reading project began to examine the origin of a question, distinct outcomes, evidence quality, and remaining research together. The investment project began to examine interpretation, expectancy, loss structure, samples, reproducibility, and risk limits together. The dispute project began to examine facts, evidence, applicability, authority, and remedies together.

Dotdotgod memory areas define the role of each document. Traceability expresses how knowledge moves toward another judgment or action. A missing relationship becomes a visible gap, and filling that gap improves the memory structure itself.

This cycle expanded the reading project's research questions, increased the number of dimensions used in investment judgment, and turned a dispute into answerable questions and institution-specific actions. Connected project memory creates a knowledge map in which learning and action continuously update one another.
